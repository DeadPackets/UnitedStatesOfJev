The Desk, in colour

One screen, no scrolling. Same turn, same numbers, the same icons and the same progressive disclosure — hover a ledger, hover a seat, pin what matters. What changes is how far the colour goes.

  1. Variant A

    Semantic accents

    Paper stays exactly as it is. Colour only carries meaning: one hue per resource on its icon, its bar, its delta, its price-tag row and its wire entry. The quietest of the three.

  2. Variant B

    Two surfaces

    The stage goes dark: the floor and the whip count sit on slate with lit faction seats, while the desk and the rail stay paper. The ledger strip becomes the HUD band across the top of both.

  3. Variant C

    Era palette

    The pack paints the page: warm marble paper, terracotta accent, gold rules, a laurel in the masthead and the price tag closed with a wax seal. The loudest, with the paper held under 0.04 chroma.

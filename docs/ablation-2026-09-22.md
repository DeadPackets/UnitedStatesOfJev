## What mattered

**L4 (Wikidata dates and colors) mattered most.** It fixed every Rome `leader_alive` failure (2 of 2 in C0 to 0 of 2) and was the only single lever that produced the Wikidata party colors for Egypt. It cost $0.0006 and 3 s per build. Its weak point is the lookup, not the prompt: label search returned the wrong Lepidus (d. 152 BC), a 1996-born "Octavian", and dateless items for Moussa, Khaled Ali and Suleiman; Luna ignored the bad rows. L2 (facts sheet) came second: it also removed the dead leaders and made Egypt name Morsi in the start, for $0.0037 and 8 s. ALL matched L4 on Rome and beat every single lever on Egypt (1 error, `dissolution` only).

**L1, L3 and L5 did nothing for accuracy.** L1 moved the dated storylet onto real dates, but the calendar it picked (20 days from Feb 24 or Mar 1) put the Ides on turn 6 or 15, so `ides_turn` stayed a coin flip; the schema change also made Luna write patron tags with underscores in 3 of 3 runs (38, 38, 26 ref errors vs 1 in C0). L3 caught Pompey twice and fixed him, but its "leader appears in sources" check rejected two real Egyptian leaders (Makhioun, Badawi) that the sources do not name, and the retry could not fix that. L5's review listed 32 to 52 claims per pack, mostly seat counts and chamber size the sources cannot confirm; the repair then hedged: L5-rome-2 ended with Caesar and Cicero each leading two factions, and ALL-rome-1 put "You hold a fictional seat" into every premise (11 disclaimers, the only disclaimers in the experiment besides one bio).

**ALL costs $0.022 per build and 76 s, against $0.005 and 28 s for C0**, a 4.4x cost and 2.7x time for a drop from 3.0 to 1.7 factual errors. L4 alone gets 1.7 errors on average (1.0 on Rome) for $0.0056 and 31 s. Recommendation: ship L4 plus L2, skip L5, keep L3 only for the alive-on-start-date check with sheet dates, and fix L1 by having the code set the calendar (start date and unit) rather than the model.

## Method notes and deviations

- Lead-only sources go to the frame call in every condition. The extra sections (Rome: Assassination "Preceding events" and "Conspiracy", Roman Senate "Senate of the Roman Republic"; Egypt: Morsi "Presidency", parliamentary election "Results" and "Dissolution, reinstatement decree") go only to the side calls of L2, L3 and L5 (facts extraction, leader-in-sources check, review), so the frame prompt differs from C0 only by the lever. Source chars counts what the run received in total.
- L4's plan step (Luna, `{people, parties}`) ran once per scenario in `fetch-ab.ts`; its cost ($0.0002) and time (2.3 s) are added to every L4 and ALL run. Wikidata dates came from `wbgetentities` because the SPARQL endpoint returned HTML on the first burst.
- The Rome region regex had a word-boundary bug (`sicil\b` did not match "Sicily"). It was fixed and all 21 saved packs were rescored offline (`rescore.ts`); no model call was repeated. Six runs lost a spurious `regions_era` failure. The rule text did not change.
- `morsi_seat` is a name-mention check: "You won the presidency" without the name fails it. `no_real_member` in Egypt fired only on the surname Haddad; in Rome it fired on Piso, Trebonius and the praenomen Decimus.
- The baseline schema holds one dated storylet, so "declaration then referendum in order" could not be tested; `const_dated` and `const_turn` test the one slot.
- Total spend: $0.225 on the 21 runs plus $0.0004 for the two plan calls, of the $2.50 budget.

# Factual checklist (frozen before any ablation run)

Applied by code (`ablation.ts`, `factCheck()`) to every pack identically. One line per item; a run's "factual errors" is the count of failed items, listed by id.

## Rome, 44 BC (start of term assumed before the Ides; reference date -0044-01-01 when the pack has no calendar)

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| leader_alive | no faction `leader` matches the dead-before-March-44 list: Pompey (not Sextus), Cato, Crassus, Clodius, Sulla, Marius, Catiline, Bibulus, Scipio, Milo, Curio, Labienus, Cinna, Lucullus, Hortensius, Ahenobarbus/Domitius |

| cicero_group | a faction whose leader is Cicero, or whose name or ideology mentions Cicero |
| antony_placed | "Anton" appears in a leader, a start seat_title/premise, or a problem |
| ides_dated | the dated storylet mentions the Ides, Caesar, the Curia, Pompey's theatre, assassination, conspiracy, daggers or March |
| ides_turn | the dated storylet's turn is 16..20 (last quarter of 20). Under L1 the turn is computed from `date` and `calendar` |
